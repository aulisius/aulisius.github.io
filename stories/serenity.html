my serenity

Written on October 22th, 2026

my serenity, I'm searching for you
in the plains, the dunes, the peaks

in the dead leaves, the dark caves, the shallow creek
in the farms, the towns, the concrete

my serenity, will i ever find you
all i desire is a moment beside you

my serenity,
is heaven, holding you for an eternity?
